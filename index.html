Good questions

A clear starting point.

Can schools use their existing cameras?

Yes. The first step is to test suitable cameras, phones, microphones and editing computers. Recording and edited uploads offer a simple entry point; live or remote production follows when equipment, connectivity and permissions support it.

Do schools have to stop using SuperSport?

No. Schools can publish directly and negotiate wider licensing where their rights allow it. Each school’s existing contracts, event rights and sponsor obligations are reviewed before deciding the distribution plan.

Will this automatically reduce school fees?

No. Verified net income can support affordability, bursaries or other approved school priorities. Any fee decision belongs to the school’s governing structures. Media participation is voluntary and is not a condition of access to education.

How can local clubs participate?

A school hub can provide approved production services to nearby clubs. Written terms cover fixtures, filming rights, supervision, costs, sponsor placements and how revenue is shared.

How are pupils protected?

School productions require adult supervision, permissions and publication approval. The proposed model uses age-appropriate commercial rules, controlled access and a takedown process. Recording for review replaces live publication when restrictions cannot reliably be met.

Can I subscribe or watch matches now?

The school network is being developed. Channels, viewing schedules and subscriptions will open as schools approve their participation and content arrangements. You can enquire about a school partnership or sponsor opportunity below.
